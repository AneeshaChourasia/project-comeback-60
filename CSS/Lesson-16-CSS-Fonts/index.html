1. Font Family

Serif fonts have small decorative lines at the ends of characters. They provide a classic and traditional appearance and are commonly used in books, newspapers, and formal documents.

Sans-serif fonts do not have decorative lines. They look clean, modern, and easy to read, making them the most popular choice for websites and mobile applications.

Monospace fonts give every character the same width. They are widely used in programming, code editors, and terminals because they improve code readability.

Cursive fonts are designed to look like handwritten text. They are mainly used for invitations, greeting cards, logos, and creative designs rather than long paragraphs.

Fantasy fonts are decorative and artistic. They are useful for posters, banners, game titles, and creative projects but should be avoided for regular website content.
